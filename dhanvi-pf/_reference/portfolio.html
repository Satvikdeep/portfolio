Gujarat's Most Beautiful Shores
What she wrote ↓
"Gujarat's coast is one of India's great natural inheritances, vast, varied, and gleaming by the light that makes every hour on its shores feel like the golden one."
Each beach got its own paragraph — a distinct personality, a distinct voice. Shivrajpur gets quiet reverence. Somnath gets sacred awe. Pingleshwar gets wonder. The challenge: 8 beaches, 8 moods, one consistent voice. Plus the closing line that had to carry everything:
"Gujarat's beaches have it all — the warm sand, the sacred shores, the moments that carry the scent of incense and salt together. Come, feel it for yourself."

Featured Project

Gujarat Tourism — Magazine Spreads

About the client
Tourism Corporation of Gujarat Limited (TCGL) — the official tourism arm of the Government of Gujarat. One of India's most storied state tourism bodies.
The brief
Write travel editorial copy for a magazine double spread covering Gujarat's coastline — 8 beaches, each with their own character and story.
Her work
· Hero headline & standfirst copy
· Individual beach descriptions (8 unique voices)
· Closing call-to-action line
· Multiple layout variations for print
Shivrajpur Beach

"A few kilometres from the sacred city of Dwarka, this is where the Arabian Sea presents itself in colours most languages cannot name."

Somnath Beach

"This is a beach for standing still. At dusk, with temple bells in the salt air, the experience becomes something for which most of us have no words."

Pingleshwar Beach

"Golden sand, endless horizon, and the company of migratory birds that arrive each season as though they know something the rest of us don't."

Luni Beach

"Be surrounded by water on all sides. The sand here is soft, the evening breeze cool and unhurried, and the only sound is the kind that reminds you what silence actually feels like."

"Gujarat's beaches have it all — the warm sand, the sacred shores, the moments that carry the scent of incense and salt together. Come, feel it for yourself." — Closing line, Gujarat Tourism Magazine Spread
